# Reports And Operational Updates

## Outcome And Intake

Enable the reader to understand status, explain material deviation, and assign follow-up. Ask for reporting period, audience, decisions needed, KPI definitions, actual/plan sources, reporting scope, previous baseline, owners, known limitations and output format. Unknowns remain `xx`; no invented targets, completion percentages, risk scores or delivery dates.

## Claim

Write one complete sentence identifying what changed, whether it matters, and the supported implication. Distinguish signal from noise and observed result from forecast. "Monthly results" is not a head message. Do not claim a cause from a correlation without evidence.

## Logic

Structure the ledger as overall status, material changes, explanations, risk/forecast, actions and decisions. The governing message is the report's conclusion. Separate routine monitoring from exceptions requiring attention. Parallel units must use the same time window, KPI definition and level of aggregation.

## Purpose Patterns

| Intent | Required inputs | Native starting point | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Summarize status | Defined measures, scope, actual/plan values | matrix/labeled | Status labels have a documented threshold |
| Explain a trend | Ordered dates and consistent measure | line-graph/labeled | No mixed cadence or undisclosed missing periods |
| Compare units | Comparable denominators and period | horizontal-bar-graph/labeled | Same axis, category order, and units |
| Reconcile a deviation | Opening, signed contributions, closing | water-fall/labeled | Totals reconcile, contributors have evidence |
| Show composition | Complete categories and totals | add-vertical-bar-graph/labeled | Absolute and proportional interpretations are distinct |
| Track delivery | Periods, tasks, owners, actual progress | gantt-chart/labeled | Progress is measured or explicitly assumed |
| Explain risks | Assessment criteria, likelihood/impact, owner | matrix/labeled | Qualitative categories are not fabricated numeric scores |
| Assign follow-up | Action, owner, timing and closure criterion | list-enumeration/labeled | Every action is actionable and traceable to a finding |

Specialized forecast styling, shared axes, control limits or KPI lanes require explicit composition if the native preset does not implement them. Do not claim a generic line chart is a fully compliant statistical process-control plot.

## Draw

Use stable positions and scales across periods and organizations. Actuals, targets and forecasts must have a consistent declared visual grammar. Highlight the material exception, not every number. Keep actual-value labels, units and source locators readable. Comparisons should use common axes; a change in axis limits must be disclosed rather than visually exaggerating movement.

Align supporting tables with the chart categories. Treat blanks, zero, unavailable and not applicable as different states. Use a legend or note for missing data; do not interpolate silently. Place a figure title including the reporting period and unit directly above each view.

## Evidence And Unknowns

Retain actual source values and document transformations. A percentage requires its denominator; a change needs both baseline and comparison date. Forecasts need assumptions and a scenario label. Do not mix currencies, gross/net measures, counts and rates, or fiscal and calendar periods without normalization explicitly recorded in the evidence.

## Measure And Review

Reconcile headline values, chart totals and source data. Check actual-versus-plan attribution, dates, units, stale evidence, owners and closure criteria. Read headlines alone to recover the report's status and required response. Inspect text, charts and table cells in the actual exported PPTX. Structural and declared-provenance checks do not establish source authenticity or causal truth.

## Delivery And Refinements

Deliver an editable report with source notes and a distinct action list. Offer a sharper exception view, a documented forecast sensitivity, or a consistent series for the next reporting cycle. Keep limitations visible instead of filling gaps with synthetic results.
